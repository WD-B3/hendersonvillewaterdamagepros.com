How to Shut Off Your Water Main in Hendersonville, TN

When a pipe lets go, the next minute matters. Finding the main valve now takes five minutes.

By the Hendersonville Water Damage Pros team · Updated November 2026

Every home has a main water shutoff, and most people do not know where it is until a pipe breaks. Finding it now, and checking that it turns, can save a flooded floor. This guide explains where to look, how to turn it, and what to do if it fails.

There are usually two valves. One is inside the house where the line enters. The other is outside, near the meter, and that one belongs to the utility.

Where to look inside

The main shutoff is usually where the water line enters the house. In many homes that is near the water heater, in a basement, a garage, a utility room or a crawl space. In homes on a slab it may be in a closet or by an exterior wall. Look for a pipe coming up through the floor or wall with a valve on it.

  • Check near the water heater and the water softener, if there is one.
  • Look in the garage and in utility closets.
  • Follow the line from the outdoor meter box toward the house.
  • In a basement, look on the front wall facing the street.
  • Check the home inspection report or closing documents, which often list it.

Types of valves and how to turn them

A ball valve has a lever handle. A quarter turn so the handle sits across the pipe closes it. A gate valve has a round wheel handle, and you turn it clockwise several full turns. Gate valves can stick or break if forced.

Test it and tag it

Turn the valve closed once while no one needs water and open a faucet to confirm the flow stops. Then reopen it slowly. A valve that is stiff or leaking when turned should be replaced by a plumber before an emergency. Put a tag or a label on the valve so everyone in the house can find it.

Know where your electric panel is as well, since a burst pipe near outlets may call for shutting off power. If you are not sure whether it is safe to reach the panel, call the 24/7 line and the dispatcher will walk you through it.

What to do after you shut it off

Open the lowest faucet to drain the line, take photos of the damage, move valuables and call for help. The water extraction page explains what the crew will do about standing water. If the valve will not turn, call a plumber and the utility.

Practice drill for the household

Everyone who lives in the house should be able to shut off the water. Walk through it together once a year. Show where the valve is, which way it turns and what to do next. Children old enough to know about emergencies can learn this too.

  1. Locate the valve and confirm it is not blocked by storage.
  2. Turn it closed and open a faucet to confirm the flow stops.
  3. Reopen it slowly to avoid a pressure surge.
  4. Check for drips at the valve and note if it feels stiff.
  5. Label it with a tag and add the location to your emergency list.

Special cases

Some homes have a separate shutoff for each fixture, which can stop a leak at a single sink or toilet. Look for small oval or round handles under fixtures. If the leak is at the water heater, close the cold water inlet valve on top of the tank and turn off the power or gas supply as the label directs.

Homes with a well use a different setup, so look for the pressure tank and the pump breaker instead.

Keep tools within reach

If your valve needs a wrench or a key, store the tool next to it. A shutoff you cannot turn in an emergency is no better than one you cannot find. Keep a flashlight nearby too, since many shutoffs are in dark corners of basements and garages.

After the water is off

Once the water is stopped, drying is next. Professional crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard for water damage restoration, and it is reasonable to ask a company whether it does.

What to know in Hendersonville

Hendersonville Utility District provides water service in the Hendersonville area. The meter and the valve beside it belong to the utility, so call the district before turning the meter-side valve.

Local questions

Where is the main water shutoff in my Hendersonville house?

It is usually where the water line enters, near the water heater or in a basement, garage or utility room. Check closing documents and the outdoor meter box line.

What if my main valve will not turn?

Do not force it. Call a plumber, and call Hendersonville Utility District if you need the water shut off at the meter.

Should I shut off the water when I go on vacation?

Many people do, especially in cold weather. Shutting off the main reduces the risk of a leak running while you are away.
