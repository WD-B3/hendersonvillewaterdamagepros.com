Wet Mattress or Sofa After a Leak: Hendersonville, TN Guide

Soft furniture holds water deep inside. Whether you can save it depends mostly on what kind of water it was.

By the Hendersonville Water Damage Pros team · Updated November 2026

Mattresses and sofas soak up water like sponges and dry slowly. Whether they can be saved depends on the type of water, how long they stayed wet and what the filling is made of. This guide helps you decide, and it explains what to do in the first hour.

The short version: clean water caught within a day can sometimes be dried out of furniture. Dirty water usually means replacement.

First steps for wet furniture

Move wet furniture off the wet floor and onto a dry surface or foil-wrapped blocks. Keep upholstery away from direct heat. Photograph everything before you move it, because the insurance file starts with those pictures.

  1. Identify the water source and make sure it is stopped.
  2. Take photos or video of the furniture and the surrounding floor.
  3. Remove cushions and bedding and stand them on edge in a dry, ventilated area.
  4. Blot surface water with towels. Do not rub.
  5. Use fans and a dehumidifier in the room to start drying.
  6. Call for help if more than a few pieces are soaked or the room is wet.

Mattresses

Foam, innerspring and hybrid mattresses all hold water deep inside. A mattress that was only splashed with clean water and dried the same day may be fine. A mattress that was soaked, left damp for days or hit by dirty water is usually replaced, because moisture inside the core is hard to remove and odor can return.

Sofas and upholstered furniture

Sofas have wood or metal frames and padded cushions. The frame can often be dried and the cushions replaced. Fabric that was soaked with dirty water cannot be sanitized reliably. Leather can crack as it dries unevenly, and it needs prompt attention.

When the room is wet too

If the furniture got wet, the floor and walls probably did too. Wet carpet under a sofa and wet pad can hold water for days. See carpet water damage restoration for what happens to the floor, and the service area page for where dispatch covers. If the water was contaminated, keep soft furniture out of the room and do not try to clean it yourself.

Drying small soft items

Pillows, throw cushions and small rugs dry faster than large furniture. Remove them from the wet room, spread them in a ventilated area and use a fan. Wash fabric items when the care label allows, and replace pillows and stuffed items that stayed wet for a day or more.

  • Stand cushions on edge so air reaches both sides.
  • Keep items out of direct sun, which can fade and warp them.
  • Do not use a heat gun or high heat, which can melt foam.
  • Replace anything that smells sour after it feels dry.

Protecting the floor underneath

Furniture sitting on wet carpet can stain it with rust or dye. Put foil or plastic tabs under legs, or lift pieces onto blocks. Photograph the stains because they may matter for the claim. The longer furniture sits on wet floor, the more likely it is to be a total loss.

When to let it go

It is hard to throw away an expensive mattress or couch, but a piece that stays damp inside can hold odor and growth for months. If you are unsure, ask the crew to take readings. A reading that stays high after days of drying is a good reason to replace the item and put the money toward a claim.

Documenting furniture for insurance

Photograph each piece from several angles before moving it. Capture labels, tags and serial numbers where visible. Write down where and when you bought it if you remember, and keep any receipts. A short spreadsheet or note listing items, ages and replacement costs makes the personal property section of a claim much easier to complete.

Standards for contaminated items

The restoration standard, ANSI/IICRC S500, treats contaminated water differently from clean water, and soft furniture that absorbed contaminated water is generally discarded rather than cleaned.

What to know in Hendersonville

The Tennessee Department of Commerce and Insurance advises policyholders to take photos or video of damage and to keep notes of each conversation with the insurer. For personal property such as a mattress or sofa, list each item with age and a rough value.

If the leak was from a pipe or an appliance, your policy may treat the water damage differently from the failed part, so ask your insurer how it handles contents.

Local questions

Can a wet mattress dry out on its own?

A mattress that is only damp on the surface may dry with air and a dehumidifier. A soaked mattress usually has water deep inside and may keep odor even after it feels dry.

Will insurance replace furniture ruined by a leak?

Often for sudden leaks, but policies differ. Ask your insurer how personal property is covered, and keep photos and a list.

Is it safe to keep a couch that got wet from a sewer backup?

No. Contaminated water cannot be reliably removed from upholstery, and the safe choice is to discard it.
