Water Damage Restoration in Cages Bend, Hendersonville, TN

Cages Bend mixes lake homes, condos and open land. Here is how water and sewer losses are handled for each.

☎ (629) 205-8280

Cages Bend is named in the City of Hendersonville land use plan, which also names the Cages Road Peninsula as one of the three peninsulas holding most of the city's single-family land. The plan describes an eastern peninsula there where agricultural and unimproved land is concentrated.

That mix of homes, condos and open land shapes the questions people ask. This page covers sewer backups, condo losses and ordinary plumbing failures, and it avoids claims about flood history that no source supports.

Condos and private sewer systems

The city plan states that Tanasi Shores, a condo development on Cages Bend, is served by a private sewer system. That matters when a drain backs up, because the first call may belong with the association or the system operator and not with the city utility.

If you live on a private system, ask your association who maintains the lines, and tell them before cleanup starts.

When the water is contaminated

A sewer backup is not clean water. Treat it as contaminated, keep people and pets out, and do not try to dry it with household fans or an ordinary vacuum.

  • Stop using sinks, toilets and showers that drain to the affected line.
  • Keep children and pets out of the area.
  • Do not touch wet materials without protective gear.
  • Photograph the damage from a safe distance.
  • Call for professional cleanup and tell the dispatcher what the water looks like.

What cleanup involves

Contaminated water calls for protective gear, removal of porous materials that absorbed it, cleaning and disinfection, and drying. Carpet, pad, insulation and lower drywall are often removed, because they cannot be reliably cleaned. The sewage backup cleanup page explains each step and the standard crews follow, ANSI/IICRC S500.

Ordinary plumbing and appliance leaks

Most water losses in condos and homes alike begin with a supply line, a water heater or a dishwasher, and they are handled as clean water if caught quickly. Shut off the fixture valve, lift belongings off the floor and call.

In a condo, water can travel to units below. Tell neighbors and management, and keep your own photos.

Records for an association or landlord

If you rent, or your building has an association, send them the same photos and notes you keep for yourself. A short written log of what you saw, when you reported it and who responded avoids disputes later. Ask the association whether it requires a particular process for shared lines, walls or common areas before work begins.

Crawl spaces and low areas

If a home has a crawl space, wet insulation and damp soil can follow a leak or heavy rain. Do not enter a flooded crawl space. A restoration crew checks it from the access point and measures moisture in the framing. See crawl space water damage.

Local context for Cages Bend

Old Hickory Lake and its tributaries have floodplains, according to the city plan, which also notes small wetland pockets near the lakeshore. The plan does not say which Cages Bend addresses, if any, are in a mapped flood area.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. This page does not state a flood zone for the area. To check a specific address, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center or ask the city building and codes office.

Questions about water damage in Cages Bend

Who do I call first for a sewer backup in a Cages Bend condo?

If your building is on a private sewer system, as the city plan says Tanasi Shores is, start with your association or the system operator. Call the restoration line for cleanup.

Is sewage water safe to clean myself?

No. It is contaminated, and it needs protective gear, proper cleaning and disposal of absorbed materials. Keep people and pets away and call a professional.

Will insurance cover a sewer backup?

Many homeowners policies exclude sewer or drain backup unless an endorsement was added. Check your policy and ask your insurer.

Can you help if my crawl space has standing water?

A crew can assess it from the access point and dry the structure. Do not enter a flooded crawl space yourself.
