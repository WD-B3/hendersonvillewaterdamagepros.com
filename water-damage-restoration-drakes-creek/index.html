Water Damage Restoration in Drakes Creek, Hendersonville, TN

Homes near Drakes Creek face heavy-rain questions as well as pipes and appliances. This page separates what is documented from what is not.

☎ (629) 205-8280

Drakes Creek runs through central Hendersonville, and the city's land use plan says FEMA maps show areas along it as prone to flooding. A neighborhood guide describes the Drakes Creek and City Center area as anchored by Drakes Creek Park, with a wide range of housing types.

This page covers storm and plumbing water damage for homes near the creek and nearby neighborhoods such as Colonial Acres. It does not claim that any particular street has flooded.

What the sources say about the area

The city plan states that FEMA maps show floodplain along Drakes Creek and Station Camp Creek, and that streams in the city flow southeast toward Old Hickory Lake and the Cumberland River. It also says Drakes Creek Park and Friendship Island have greenways.

A buyer guide places Colonial Acres between State Route 386 and US 31E, with detached homes that range from the 1960s and 1970s to the mid-2000s, and Drakes Creek Park inside the neighborhood.

A regional storm in 2024

NewsChannel 5 reported on May 9, 2024 that overnight storms caused flooding in Sumner County and that Drakes Creek overflowed. The report did not name Hendersonville neighborhoods.

That is regional context. It does not show what happened on any street here.

Storm water and flood water

Water that enters from outside can carry soil, debris and contaminants. Treat it as contaminated until a professional says otherwise.

  1. Stay out of moving or deep water, and do not walk through it to reach a breaker.
  2. Keep people and pets out of the affected area.
  3. Do not run household fans or an ordinary vacuum on flood water.
  4. Photograph the damage when it is safe to do so.
  5. Call emergency services for immediate danger and the dispatch line for cleanup.

Plumbing and appliance water

Homes of every age can lose water to a split pipe, a failed hose or a leaking tank. A neighborhood's age is not evidence that any of these is more likely, so this page does not make that claim.

See burst pipe water damage and appliance leak cleanup for the response to each.

Questions to ask before you approve work

After a storm, many households in the same area call at once, and it helps to compare plans calmly. A clear scope answers the following.

  • Which rooms are in the scope, and how many drying days are planned?
  • What moisture readings will be recorded at each visit?
  • Which materials will be removed, and why?
  • What is excluded and billed separately, such as rebuild work?
  • Will the paperwork be in a form your insurer can review?

Drying after storm water

After extraction, wet carpet pad, insulation and the lower part of drywall are often removed, and air movers and dehumidifiers dry the rest. The flood damage cleanup page explains the steps and the standard crews follow, ANSI/IICRC S500. Flood damage usually needs a separate flood policy, so ask your insurer what applies.

The city plan notes a possible Wessington Road connector with a bridge over Drakes Creek, and greenway extensions near George A. Whitten Elementary. Those are planning items and not flood findings.

Questions about water damage in Drakes Creek

Is my home near Drakes Creek in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific status was not verified. The city plan says FEMA maps show areas along Drakes Creek as prone to flooding, so check your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

Does homeowners insurance cover creek flooding?

Flood damage usually needs a separate flood policy, and most homeowners policies exclude it. Ask your insurer what applies.

Is Colonial Acres part of the service area?

Yes, Colonial Acres is inside the city between State Route 386 and US 31E, according to a buyer guide. Give your address when you call to confirm coverage.

How soon should cleanup start after heavy rain gets in?

As soon as it is safe. The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth.
