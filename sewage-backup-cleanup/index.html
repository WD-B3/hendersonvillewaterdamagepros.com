Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in Hendersonville, TN

Sewage water is contaminated and should be treated that way. Stay out of it and call the 24/7 line.

☎ (629) 205-8280

If sewage is coming up through a floor drain, toilet, tub or sink in your Hendersonville home, treat it as an emergency and keep everyone out of the water. Stop using water in the house, keep children and pets away, and call the 24/7 line. Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel on dry ground.

Sewage backup cleanup is the safe removal and disinfecting of contaminated water that has entered a building from a drain or sewer line. It is Category 3 water under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which means it is presumed to carry harmful organisms. Crews wear protective gear and use containment where needed.

Sewage backup: what to do first

Time and safety are both at stake. The first minutes should be spent protecting people, not saving belongings.

  1. Keep people and pets out of the contaminated area.
  2. Stop using sinks, tubs, toilets, dishwashers and washing machines, since they add water to the backup.
  3. Turn off power to the affected rooms only if the panel is dry and reachable.
  4. Open windows if it is safe, to ventilate.
  5. Do not try to clean the water with a household vacuum or by hand.
  6. Call the 24/7 line and, if the main line may be blocked outside your house, the utility.

Do not enter standing sewage water

Sewage can carry bacteria and viruses. Do not wade in, do not touch affected items without protection, and wash hands after any contact. For health questions, ask a physician.

Where backups come from

Backups have a few common causes. A clog in your own line, which is the homeowner's responsibility. A blockage or failure in the public main, which is the utility's. A sump or ejector pump failure in a basement bathroom. Heavy rain overwhelming a sewer system. Tree roots in older clay lines.

Hendersonville Utility District provides sanitary sewer service in the greater Hendersonville area. If your neighbors are also affected or the water is rising in more than one drain, call the district as well as the restoration line, because the problem may be in the public line.

What the cleanup involves

Sewage cleanup is stricter than other water jobs because anything the water touched is considered contaminated.

  • Protective gear for the crew, including respirators, gloves and boots.
  • Extraction of the contaminated water, and disposal in line with local rules.
  • Removal of porous materials such as carpet, pad, insulation and low drywall.
  • Cleaning and disinfecting of surfaces that can be saved.
  • Air filtration and, where needed, containment barriers to protect clean rooms.
  • Structural drying and verification readings before any rebuild.

Why porous materials are removed

Carpet, pad, insulation and drywall soak up contaminated water and cannot be reliably disinfected. They are removed even when they look fine afterward. Hard surfaces such as tile, sealed concrete and framing can be cleaned and treated. Contents are sorted case by case, with non-porous items cleaned and porous items discarded.

If the water came with a larger flood, the work overlaps with flood damage cleanup. For standing clean water from a pipe, water extraction is the better fit.

Insurance and backups

Many homeowners policies exclude sewer or drain backup unless an endorsement was added. The Tennessee Department of Commerce and Insurance notes that a sewer backup is covered by a flood policy only when it is a direct result of flooding. Ask your insurer which coverage you hold, and document everything with photos before cleanup.

Condo owners on a private sewer line should read the Cages Bend page, which explains who to call first.

Questions about sewage backup cleanup in Hendersonville

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Hendersonville?

Yes. Dispatch is answered 24/7. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and the dispatcher will tell you what to do while you wait.

Can I clean sewage backup myself?

Small clean-up of a minor drain overflow on a hard floor is possible with protective gear and disinfectant. Anything that reached carpet, walls or multiple rooms should be handled by trained crews.

Is sewage backup covered by insurance?

Often only if you have a water backup endorsement or a flood policy where the backup resulted from flooding. Ask your insurer which applies.

How do I know whether the problem is in my line or the street?

If only one fixture is affected, it is often your line. If several drains back up at once or neighbors are affected, call the utility, since the public main may be blocked.
