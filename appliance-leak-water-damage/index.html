Emergency Appliance Leak Water Damage in Hendersonville, TN

Appliance hoses fail without warning. Shut off the supply, then call the 24/7 line.

☎ (629) 205-8280

If a washing machine, dishwasher, refrigerator line or similar appliance is leaking, shut off its supply valve and unplug it if you can reach the plug dry. Then call the 24/7 line. Move the appliance carefully only if the floor is not slick, since a washer full of water is heavy.

Appliance leak water damage cleanup is the removal and drying of water that came from a household appliance or its connections. The cause is usually a hose, a fitting, a drain line or a pump, which makes it different from a roof leak or a flood. Where the water reached cabinets, see the kitchen page.

When an appliance leak is an emergency

A hose burst on a washing machine can release a large amount of water in minutes, and an overflowing dishwasher can soak the kitchen floor in an hour. Leaks behind refrigerators and under dishwashers can also run for weeks without notice. Calls come in at all hours, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

After-hours calls are handled the same way as daytime calls. The dispatcher will ask what the appliance is, where the water is going, and whether anyone has shut off the supply.

Unplug before you touch

Appliances are electrical. If water is pooling around the plug or the outlet, do not touch the appliance. Cut power at the panel if you can reach it on dry ground, or call 911 if you see sparks.

What the cleanup involves

Water from a washer supply line or a dishwasher supply is usually clean at first, and treated as Category 1 under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. Drain water from a washer is Category 2. Time and contamination can raise the category.

  1. Shut off the supply, photograph the area and record the appliance model.
  2. Extract the water and pull the appliance out if needed.
  3. Check moisture in the floor, baseboards, walls and cabinet bases.
  4. Remove materials that cannot be dried, such as soaked pad or swollen particleboard.
  5. Dry the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers.
  6. Document the loss and write a rebuild scope.

Prevention that is actually useful

Replace rubber washing machine hoses with braided stainless steel ones, and check the date on the hose. Leave a little slack so hoses do not kink. Shut off the supply valve when going away for several days. Look behind the refrigerator when you move it. A small leak sensor on the floor can give an early warning, but it is not a replacement for checking connections.

Insurance and local calls

Insurers often treat a sudden appliance failure as covered, while slow leaks and maintenance issues may not be. Keep the failed hose or part if you can, since it may be asked for. The Tennessee Department of Commerce and Insurance advises filing as soon as possible and keeping notes of each conversation. Calls from ZIP 37075 and 37077 reach the same 24/7 line, and if the leak soaked the floor, the hardwood floor and carpet pages explain what happens next.

Questions about appliance leak water damage in Hendersonville

Do you handle appliance leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Hendersonville?

Yes. Dispatch is answered 24/7. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and the dispatcher will help with the shutoff.

Who pays for damage from a leaking washing machine?

That depends on your policy and the cause. Ask your insurer, and keep the failed hose or part and photos of the scene.

Is it safe to move the appliance myself?

Only if it is unplugged and the floor is not slick. A washer full of water is heavy, so avoid straining.

Can water from a washer be Category 1?

Supply water is clean, but drain water is not. The category depends on the source and how long the water sat.
