What to do before you pick a page

  • Stop the water at its source if you can, starting with the main shutoff.
  • Stay out of water near outlets, the panel or appliances.
  • Do not wade through water that may be contaminated.
  • Lift valuables off wet floors and photograph everything before moving it.
  • Call the 24/7 line if you are unsure which service fits.
