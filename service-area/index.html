Water Damage Service Area in Hendersonville, TN

Dispatch covers Hendersonville and nearby Sumner County and Nashville area towns, 24/7.

☎ (629) 205-8280

Hendersonville Water Damage Pros serves Hendersonville and the surrounding Sumner County and Nashville area. The city sits on the north side of Old Hickory Lake, with ZIP 37075 and ZIP 37077 covering most addresses. Dispatch is answered 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available.

Where you live inside the city does not change the call. A flooded basement near Indian Lake Boulevard, a burst pipe in a home off US 31E and a roof leak near State Route 386 all go to the same dispatch line. The dispatcher asks about the source of the water, the rooms affected and whether power or gas is at risk.

The first step for any address is a phone call. The dispatcher confirms the location, asks what happened and explains what to do while crews are matched to the job. The towns above are listed for reference, and the full set of services is the same everywhere.

For residents in Hendersonville, local knowledge helps. Old Hickory Lake is managed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers as part of the Cumberland River system, and the National Weather Service reports that dam releases were coordinated at least 17 times during the May 2010 flood.

That regional history is a reminder to check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your insurer about flood coverage. The city Building and Codes office at City Hall can answer permit questions before a rebuild.

If the problem is a main line or a meter, Hendersonville Utility District provides water and wastewater service and is the right call alongside the restoration line. See flood damage cleanup for rising water, and water extraction for standing water from any source.

What to Have Ready When You Call

A few details speed up the call and help the dispatcher give accurate first steps. You do not need all of them, and the dispatcher will ask for whatever is missing.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street or landmark.
  • Where the water started and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and about how long they have been wet.
  • Whether the water looks clean, cloudy or contaminated.
  • Whether power, gas or sparks are involved.
  • Whether anyone has photographed the damage yet.

Which Service Page Fits

If you are still deciding what to read while you wait, these pages cover the most common situations. Burst pipes and water heater leaks usually start with a shutoff. Basement water and sewage backups involve drains and groundwater. Ceiling leaks and wall damage involve materials that may need to come out.

Whatever the source, the same rule applies. Stop the water if you can, keep people and pets out of contaminated areas, lift valuables off wet floors and call the 24/7 line.

How Dispatch Matches a Crew

The dispatcher does not guess. The answers you give about the source, the water type and the rooms affected decide which crew and which equipment fit the job. A clean supply line break calls for extraction and drying. A sewage backup calls for protective gear and disposal. A roof leak after a storm may need temporary protection before any drying starts. Crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard for water damage restoration.

Crews are sent the same day when available, and the dispatcher explains what is realistic once the address and the situation are known. That honesty matters most during widespread storms, when many homes need help at once.

While You Wait for Help

Small actions in the first hour limit the damage. Close the main water valve if the source is a supply line. Turn off power to wet areas only when the panel is on dry ground and you can reach it safely. Move rugs, electronics and papers off wet floors, and keep children and pets away from contaminated water. Take photos before anything is moved.

Service Area Questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Hendersonville?

Dispatch covers ZIP 37075 and ZIP 37077 across the city, from the Indian Lake Boulevard area to the SR 386 and US 31E corridors, plus nearby areas such as Old Hickory 37138 and Madison 37115 and 37116. If you are not sure, call and give your address.

Is emergency water removal available in the service area?

Yes. Dispatch is answered 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Tell the dispatcher your address and the type of water damage.

Do you serve Nashville and Gallatin?

Calls from Nashville, Goodlettsville, Gallatin, Old Hickory and Madison are welcome. The dispatcher will confirm coverage for your address.
